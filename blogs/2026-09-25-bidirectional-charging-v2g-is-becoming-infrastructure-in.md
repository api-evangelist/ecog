Bidirectional charging (V2G) is becoming infrastructure in the US Electricity demand in the U.S. is rising rapidly as electrification, data centers, and AI-driven computing place new pressures on the grid. “This convergence of grid stress and EV adoption is creating an accidental infrastructure revolution,” says Charged EVs , “but we see it as an opportunity for both automakers and EV charging operators.” The aging U.S grid has reliability issues, too, particularly when there is bad weather.
