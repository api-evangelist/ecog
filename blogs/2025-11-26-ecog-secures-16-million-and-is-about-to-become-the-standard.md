Growth and impact investors GET Fund, Extantia, and Bayern Kapital invest in the Munich-based scale-up as part of a €16 million Series B funding round Financing will flow into the further standardization of the EcoG Universal Core for charging systems, which is already in use in 25,000 systems worldwide Demand for electrified logistics and fleet hubs is driving growth EcoG is entering into local partnerships for the establishment of an innovation hub for e-trucks in Bavaria
